# TruTicket Android App — Installation Guide

This guide explains how to install and update the TruTicket app on your Android phone.

> **Note:** This is a testing preview build, not a Google Play Store release.

---

## 1. Install the App (One-Time Setup)

You only need to install the APK once:

1. Open the release page on your Android phone: [TruTicket Native Android Release](https://github.com/brajesh1210/TruTicket/releases/tag/latest-android-apk)
2. Tap **`TruTicket.apk`** to download.
3. If Android prompts *"For your security, your phone is not allowed to install unknown apps from this source"*:
   - Tap **Settings** and toggle **Allow from this source**.
4. Tap **Install**, then **Open** when finished.

---

## 2. Automatic Over-The-Air (OTA) Updates

After installing the APK once, **you do not need to download new APKs for regular updates**:
- When a new update is published, simply **close and reopen the TruTicket app**.
- The app automatically downloads the latest update on launch and applies it the next time you open the app (or upon app restart).

---

## 3. Troubleshooting

- **App shows an old or broken state:** Close the app completely from your recent apps switcher and reopen it. If an issue persists, uninstall TruTicket and download a fresh APK from the link above.
- **When is a new APK required?** Most updates arrive automatically over the air. Occasionally, changes to native Android permissions or native modules require a fresh APK install. Brajesh will notify you whenever a new APK build is required.
